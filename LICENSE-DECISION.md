## Rationale

The requested policy is broad freedom to use, modify, share, fork, and update the collection while retaining credit to the original repository. CC BY 4.0 is a recognized, unmodified license for non-software creative material and requires appropriate credit, a license link, and an indication of changes. It permits commercial use and adaptations.

CC BY-SA was not selected because the request requires attribution, not mandatory relicensing of adaptations under identical terms. MIT and Apache-2.0 were not selected because this repository is primarily authored content rather than software.

## Required attribution

A reasonable attribution should identify **Human-First Prompts**, link to `https://github.com/mr-jonam/human-first-prompts`, link to CC BY 4.0, retain supplied notices, and indicate modifications. A ready-to-use example appears in [NOTICE](NOTICE).

## Incoming contributions

Contributors must have the right to submit their material under CC BY 4.0. Compatible third-party material must be identified with source, author, license, and modifications. Copied or ambiguously licensed prompt collections are not accepted.
