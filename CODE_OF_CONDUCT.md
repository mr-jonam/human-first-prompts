# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique the contribution, not the contributor. Welcome different levels of experience, languages, backgrounds, and access needs. Do not harass, demean, threaten, dox, or publish another person's private information.

## Project spaces

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents this project. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact options on the GitHub profile of [`mr-jonam`](https://github.com/mr-jonam). Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed in context and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Attribution

This compact policy is original to this repository and follows common open-community expectations. It is not intended to replace platform rules or applicable law.
